# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The project founder, the Steel team, and engineers reviewing a physical companion for a persistent agent.

## Product purpose

Steel Marty is the companion name. It comes from martensite. The founder supplied a faceted character with blue eyes.

A standalone HTML design report. Move from visual exploration and source research to a proposed system, a prototype plan, and testable outcomes.

## Brand personality

Tactile, curious, exact. Plain technical language with dry humor and varied rhythm. The user delegates creative direction.

## Anti-references

Avoid a sales landing page, an endless dashboard, generic robot imagery, and unsupported claims about features or results.

## Design principles

- Start with the human experience, then explain the system.
- Mark facts, proposals, and targets clearly.
- Use images for atmosphere and diagrams for mechanisms.
- Make the full report portable and useful offline.
- Make every control explain something real.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Use readable contrast, semantic structure, descriptive image text, keyboard access, and reduced motion. Support phones and print.

## Pairing and distribution

Support a proposed household or team space with people, agents, and devices. Use QR pairing and a physical confirmation. Explore direct Wi-Fi and a Bluetooth Low Energy phone relay. Keep device ownership, agent access, and permission to approve separate. One agent can serve several devices. The multi-device pilot follows the first single-device prototype.
